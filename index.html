    Frequently asked questions

    What does Letterboxd Film Poster Generator do?

    It turns any of your Letterboxd lists into a poster: a grid of film posters with a title and captions, ready to print, set as a wallpaper or share.

    How do I get my Letterboxd data?

    Export it at letterboxd.com/data/export and you'll get a .zip file. Drop that file on this page.

    Which lists can I use?

    Any of your Letterboxd lists, plus your Watchlist, Watched films and Ratings. You can also start empty and add films by searching.

    Is it free? Do I need an account?

    It's free, and you don't need an account here. You only need a Letterboxd account to export your data.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. Your export is read in your browser and never uploaded. Your settings and recent exports are saved in this browser only.

    Can I print my poster?

    Yes. Pick a paper size (A4 to A0, Letter or 11×17) and download a print-ready PDF at up to 300 dpi, or a PNG.

    Why is a film missing its poster?

    Posters come from TMDB. When TMDB has no match for a film, it's shown as its title instead. Turn on Hide no-poster to leave those films out.

    Can I share my poster?

    Yes. Click Share… to send it to your phone's share menu, or copy it and post it to X, Bluesky, Threads, Facebook or Reddit.
